# Governance

## Introduction

Living God Ecosystem is founded on the belief that enduring institutions require transparent, accountable, and responsible governance.

Governance is not simply the process of making decisions. It is the framework through which the ecosystem protects its mission, allocates responsibility, manages resources, and evolves over time.

As Living God Ecosystem grows, governance will progressively encourage broader community participation while maintaining a clear commitment to integrity, stewardship, and long-term sustainability.

## Governance Objectives

The governance framework is designed to:

- Protect the long-term mission of Living God Ecosystem.
- Enable responsible community participation.
- Ensure transparent decision-making.
- Safeguard ecosystem resources.
- Encourage innovation through structured proposals.
- Maintain accountability across all ecosystem institutions.

During the early stages of the ecosystem, the Founder serves as the Chief Steward of Living God Ecosystem.

The Chief Steward provides strategic direction, protects the founding vision, and ensures that the ecosystem develops in accordance with its constitutional principles.

As governance matures, additional responsibilities may progressively transition to community governance mechanisms while preserving the long-term mission of the ecosystem.
## Living God Improvement Proposals (LGIPs)

Major ecosystem changes shall be documented through the Living God Improvement Proposal (LGIP) process.

LGIPs provide a transparent framework for proposing, discussing, reviewing, approving, and documenting significant architectural, technical, governance, and economic decisions.

Every accepted LGIP becomes part of the historical record of the Living God Ecosystem.

## Transparency

Living God Ecosystem is committed to transparency in governance.

The ecosystem intends to publish important governance decisions, treasury updates, major proposals, and institutional reports to encourage accountability and strengthen community trust.
