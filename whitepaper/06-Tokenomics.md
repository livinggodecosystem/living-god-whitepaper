# Economic Architecture (Tokenomics)

## Introduction

The economic architecture of Living God Coin (LGC) is designed to support long-term entrepreneurship, responsible governance, sustainable institutional growth, and meaningful real-world utility.

Rather than treating LGC solely as a tradable digital asset, Living God Ecosystem views the token as the economic foundation of a broader entrepreneurial infrastructure.

Every component of the economic architecture has been designed with a long-term perspective, ensuring that value is created through innovation, participation, and sustainable ecosystem development.

The objective is not simply to distribute tokens.

The objective is to build an economy capable of supporting entrepreneurs, strengthening communities, and creating opportunities for generations to come.

## Design Principles

The Living God Economy is guided by the following principles:

### Utility Before Speculation

The primary purpose of LGC is to enable participation across the Living God Ecosystem through practical applications and services.

### Long-Term Sustainability

Economic decisions should strengthen the ecosystem over decades rather than optimize for short-term market activity.

### Responsible Stewardship

Every token represents a valuable ecosystem resource and must be managed responsibly.

### Community Participation

The long-term success of Living God Ecosystem depends on an active and engaged global community.

### Entrepreneurial Empowerment

The economy exists to support builders, innovators, and entrepreneurs who create lasting value.

## Maximum Supply

Living God Coin has a permanently fixed maximum supply of:

15,000,000 LGC

No additional tokens shall be created beyond this maximum supply except through a future governance process explicitly approved according to the governance framework.

The fixed supply supports predictability, transparency, and long-term confidence in the ecosystem.
